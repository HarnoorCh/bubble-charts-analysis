A spreadsheet showing weekly data from 2019W52 to 2020W16 with columns: report_week, PET, AWT, AVT, TTP, DT, DT > 45 mins, rider late > 10 mins, % EPT model usage, EPT.

- 2020W15: ~20.63
- 2020W16: ~15.14

**Why:** ~50% decline likely reflects COVID-19 impact starting around late March 2020 (W13–W14). Most recent rows were highlighted pink/red in the spreadsheet.
